The Way of the House

Respect. Sweat. Then talk.

SparrClub isn't a "fitness center" — it's a student environment built around discipline and respect. Five principles guide every member who walks through our door.

  1. — 01 Coaches are called Coach. You're here to learn from them — show that with how you speak.
  2. — 02 Sharing is caring. Don't hog the heavy bag. Plan circuits, rotate, make room.
  3. — 03 Keep it clean. Wipe it down when you're done — nobody wants somebody else's sweat.
  4. — 04 Sparring is practice, not showing off. Don't try to take anyone's head off.
  5. — 05 Re‑rack your weights. Spotters mandatory. Safety is the priority.

House Rules

Read the room. Earn the room.

A few rules keep this place worth showing up to. They're not bureaucracy — they're the difference between a gym and a club. We expect every member, from day one to year ten, to live by them.

/ THE GOLDEN RULE

No sparring without the coach's consent.

This is a student environment and safety is the priority. Breaking this rule is grounds for immediate termination of your gym privileges. No exceptions, no second chances.

Strict — Zero tolerance
/ 02

Yes Coach, No Coach

Boxing culture is built on discipline and respect. Address coaches by their title — you're here to learn from them.

/ 03

Sharing is caring

Don't hog the heavy bag for 12 rounds. Plan circuits, rotate after one or two rounds, make room.

/ 04

Keep it clean

Wipe down every piece of equipment when you finish. Nobody wants to clinch on somebody else's sweat.

/ 05

No battering in sparring

Sparring is practice, not a fight. Don't try to take your opponent's head off. The goal is to learn, not to hurt.

/ 06

Re‑rack your weights

Every lifter must re‑rack their dumbbells and plates. Use a spotter — ask a coach if you don't have one.

/ 07

Attire matters

Sneakers, socks, athletic wear. SparrClub wear preferred. No jeans, no sandals, no bare feet on the floor.

/ 08

Under 18? No ring.

Members below 18 can train with bags and dummies, but the ring is off‑limits for sparring sessions.
